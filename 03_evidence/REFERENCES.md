# Key research citations and contextual sources

This is a curated reading index from the existing research, not a new systematic review. The machine-readable source index contains additional citations and exact referring files. A paper's inclusion does not imply that its code, parameters or complete model were reproduced.

1. Bachinger, J. & Zander, P. (2007). *ROTOR, a tool for generating and evaluating crop rotations for organic farming systems*. European Journal of Agronomy 26(2), 130–143. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eja.2006.09.002 . User-provided original: `../../repo/7206_Bachinger.pdf`.
2. Schönhart, M., Schmid, E. & Schneider, U. A. (2011). *CropRota – A crop rotation model to support integrated land use assessments*. European Journal of Agronomy 34(4), 263–277. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eja.2011.02.004 .
3. Grewer et al. (2026). *Crop models as decision support tools in digital agriculture: The APSIM-based RotationARM tool in Australia*. Computers and Electronics in Agriculture 247, 111725. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2026.111725 . Citation carried forward from the research merge notes.
4. Davis et al. (2012). *Increasing Cropping System Diversity Balances Productivity, Profitability and Environmental Health*. PLOS ONE 7(10), e47149. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0047149 . Historic management-system comparison; not a current farmer profit forecast.
5. Lazicki, P. A., Liebman, M. & Wander, M. M. (2016). *Root Parameters Show How Management Alters Resource Distribution and Soil Quality in Conventional and Low-Input Cropping Systems in Central Iowa*. PLOS ONE 11(10), e0164209. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0164209 . S1/S2 soil/root data; CC BY.
6. Mulik, K. (2015). *Economic Impacts of Diversified Cropping Systems*. AAEA/WAEA selected conference paper. https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/205805 . Historic budget estimates; retain original copyright/noncommercial-copy notice; not labelled a peer-reviewed journal article.
7. Saxton, K. E. & Rawls, W. J. (2006). *Soil Water Characteristic Estimates by Texture and Organic Matter for Hydrologic Solutions*. https://doi.org/10.2136/sssaj2005.0117 . Retention functions are estimated priors, not measured local hydraulic properties.
8. Nichols and contributors, *maRsden* author dataset, pinned commit `01476a18c01115f70f154cf7e94c1951205fbda1`. https://github.com/vanichols/maRsden/tree/01476a18c01115f70f154cf7e94c1951205fbda1 . MIT notice retained.
9. Practical Farmers of Iowa. *Cover Crop Effect on Soil Water* (2022 trials). https://practicalfarmers.org/resources/research-reports/cover-crop-effect-on-soil-water/ . Published treatment summaries, not acquired raw strip/sensor observations.

## Data, markets and macro context

- NASA POWER: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/ ; Earthdata: https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/ . Exact extraction parameters and dates remain in source receipts.
- USDA NASS / CDL: https://www.nass.usda.gov/ ; NRCS soil data: https://sdmdataaccess.nrcs.usda.gov/ .
- USDA AMS Market News: https://mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov/ . Market reports are dated context, not verified farm sale contracts.
- Iowa State University agricultural budgets: https://www.extension.iastate.edu/agdm/ . Estimated enterprise budgets are not field cash accounts.
- NOAA / state weather and crop-progress references are retained in the source index and acquisition receipts.

Independent news reporting has not been established here as a validated numerical model input. Do not infer measured effects of conflict, tariffs or policy from proposal scenarios. If such sources are later added, record publisher, title, event date, publication date, URL, access date and the explicit model transformation separately.
